Stuffed animals fight it out

A red cardinal named Jib Jab has one night to wake up every toy in the house. Jib Jab Smash is a platform fighting game for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV, starring a family's real stuffed animals.

Fight room by room through Story Mode, play with up to four players, and learn every move in the step-by-step lesson. On iPhone and iPad, photograph your own stuffed animal, pick its moves, and bring it into the fight.

No ads. No in-app purchases.
