Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Jib Jab Smash is a game made for kids and families. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. This page explains exactly what the game does with data.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts of its own, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. We do not run any servers that receive information from the game.

Your game save

Your progress (Story Mode, unlocked toys, the toys you made, badges, stickers and settings) is saved on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, the save is also copied to your own iCloud account so it can appear on your other Apple devices. This uses Apple's iCloud service; we cannot see or access it.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, the game reports your badges and scores to Game Center and uses it to find online matches and invite friends. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Your Game Center nickname may be shown to other players you are matched or play with. Game Center can be turned off or limited in the device's Screen Time settings.

Make a Toy: the camera and your photos

On iPhone and iPad, Make a Toy lets you take a picture of your own stuffed animal (or choose one from your photo library) and fight with it in the game. Here is exactly what happens to that picture:

  • The toy is cut out of the picture on your device, using Apple's built-in tools. The picture is never uploaded to us or to any server we run.
  • A picture with a person in it is refused, and nothing is kept from it.
  • The finished toy is kept in the game's save on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud so it appears on your family's other devices. We cannot see it.
  • When you play online, other players see your toy's shape, colors and moves, never the picture.
  • You can delete a toy at any time from MY TOYS in the game.

iOS asks for permission before the game can use the camera. The photo library is opened through Apple's own picker, which only gives the game the one picture you choose.

Playing on the same Wi-Fi

To play with other devices nearby, the game looks for other copies of Jib Jab Smash on the same Wi-Fi network. It shares only what is needed to play: a short random code or your Game Center nickname, the toy you picked, and, for a toy you made, its shape, colors and moves without its picture. Nothing is sent outside your network for this. iOS asks for permission to use the local network before this works.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. These do not identify you.

Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children. The game contains no ads, no links to websites for kids to follow, and no purchases.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
